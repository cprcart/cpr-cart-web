About CPR Cart LLC

Modern, Convenient Certification for Healthcare Professionals

CPR Cart LLC provides a convenient approach to CPR certification and renewal through the AHA HeartCode® model.

Online learning can be completed from home at your own schedule. Once the digital coursework is completed, learners attend an approximately 30-minute in-person skills session using an automated CPR Cart/RQI station.

The process is designed for convenience and flexibility. Users may receive an official AHA eCard when course requirements are completed, based on the selected course and applicable requirements. CPR Cart supports learners seeking BLS, ACLS, and PALS certification or renewal.

How It Works

Attain or renew your credentials efficiently through the structured AHA HeartCode® blended model.

1

Choose Your CPR Course

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding the AHA HeartCode® model and CPR Cart certification.

The AHA HeartCode® learning model is the American Heart Association's blended training method. It combines self-directed online learning completed by the student with a quick, approximately 30-minute in-person hands-on skills session using an automated CPR Cart/RQI system.

Once registered, you access your online HeartCode® module from home or any computer with an internet connection. The digital module covers cognitive scenarios, case evaluations, and resuscitation guidelines at your own pace before your hands-on check.

The hands-on skills session on the automated CPR Cart/RQI system lasts approximately 30 minutes. The system provides real-time audiovisual feedback to evaluate compression depth, speed, recoil, and ventilation effectiveness.

CPR Cart LLC supports American Heart Association courses including Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS), and Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) for both initial certification and renewal.

Yes. Upon successful completion of both the online AHA HeartCode® module and the automated skills session, you will receive an official American Heart Association eCard based on the selected course and applicable requirements.
